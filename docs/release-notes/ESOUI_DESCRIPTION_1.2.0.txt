NextTry StatusTracker is a configurable status tracker addon for The Elder Scrolls Online.

Features:
- Track selected friends and guild members
- Player profiles with private notes, notifications, roles and editable tags
- Optional aligned ESO role icons for raid lead, tank, healer and damage dealer
- 32-pixel class and alliance icons after character names in the tooltip
- Separate player tag assignment and global tag management
- Compact last-seen time and last known character and zone for offline players
- Automatic correction of a missing @ during manual player entry
- Extended hover tooltip
- Friend and guild roster import
- Player context menu
- Configurable sorting, grouping, styles, blink and sound
- Server-aware SavedVariables for EU, NA and PTS
- English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese

Required library:
- LibAddonMenu-2.0 r43 or newer
